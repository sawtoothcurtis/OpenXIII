# Animation reference

All the names a mod can use: [names/](names/README.md).

Generated by `oxiii mods-reference <game folder> docs/modding` (oxiii built with `--features mods`)
from the original game: the animations of the weapons seen in first person and of the
characters, with the bones of their meshes. A mod's animated model used in their place gives
the same names: `[models.<Name>.animations]` makes these animations from the model's glTF
animations, `[models.<Name>.bones]` gives these bone names to its bones (see
[expert/textures-and-models.md](expert/textures-and-models.md#animated-models)). The game's scripts play the animations by name: one
that the model does not have is not played, and the log says so.

Lengths are in frames at the sequence's rate (frames per second); notifies are the script
functions called at a fraction (0 to 1) of the sequence.
