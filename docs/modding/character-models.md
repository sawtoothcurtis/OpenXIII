# Character models

All the names a mod can use: [names/](names/README.md).

Generated by `oxiii mods-reference <game folder> docs/modding` (oxiii built with `--features mods`)
from the game's own packages and maps.

The animated models of the characters (package XIIIPersos) are named after a short or French
word, not always after the character: this page says who wears each one in the game. A
character's look is its `Mesh` (and its skin, `Skins[0]`), not its class: give
`properties = { Mesh = "XIIIPersos.SpadsM" }` to a character a mod adds (expert/characters.md, "Changing
characters"). The classes are in [characters.md](characters.md), the animations and bones of
each model in [animations.md](animations.md).

Columns: the in-game names (`PawnName`) and the classes of the characters placed in the maps
with this model (how many when more than one), the maps, the skins they wear besides the
model's own, and the classes whose default look it is. A model nobody wears in the maps is
still a valid `Mesh`, but it was only seen in cutscenes, in multiplayer or not at all: check it
in the game before using it.

**`XIIIPersos.XIIIM` is XIII himself.** It is the default `Mesh` of several classes (`XIDPawn.BaseSoldier`,
`XIDCine.Cine2`, `XIII.XIIICorpse`…): a character made from them without a `Mesh` looks like XIII.

## Names that mislead

Checked in the game, with characters added by a mod on Plage01:

- `XIDPawn.BaseSoldier` without a `Mesh`: XIII himself (`XIIIPersos.XIIIM`); the same for `XIDCine.Cine2`, `XIDCine.ChoPawn` and `XIII.XIIICorpse`: in the maps the level designer gives their characters their own `Mesh`, and those that keep this one are XIII himself in cutscenes
- `XIDPawn.FBI_Helico01a`: a uniformed guard (`XIIIPersos.GardienM`), not an FBI agent; the FBI agents are `XIIIPersos.FbiM` and `XIIIPersos.FBI2M`
- `XIIIPersos.GardienM`: a uniformed guard with a star badge ("gardien": guard), worn by the guards of Banque01 and of the prison (PRock01a, PRock03)
- `XIIIPersos.MiocheM`: a bald man in an orange vest, the killer of Plage01 ("mioche" means kid, but it is not a child)
- `XIIIPersos.KKKM`: a hooded figure in a long yellow robe (the hooded men of Sanc02a, Sanc02b, Sanc03 and PRock)
- `XIII.MitraillGuard`: invisible (`DrawType` `DT_None`): the gunner of a mounted machine gun, not a guard to place
