# Sound reference

All the names a mod can use: [names/](names/README.md).

Generated by `oxiii mods-reference <game folder> docs/modding` (oxiii built with `--features mods`)
from the original game's files.

Sounds are named `Package.Group.Name`. A sound plays only if a loaded sound bank holds its
resource; the mods build loads the bank of any sound named in a mod value (expert/sounds.md,
"Sounds from any level"). The bank column says which banks hold each sound (the shared
bank `Fix` is always loaded; a map's own bank is named after its level title).

## Character voices

A character's pain, death and other shouts are chosen by its `CodeMesh` value (and its
timbre, `NumeroTimbre`) in the onomatopoeia sets of the `Onomatopees` package. Give an added
character the `CodeMesh` of the voice it should have; the mods build loads the banks that voice
needs. Names from the game's skin list (MeshSkinList):

| CodeMesh | Character | Sounds | Banks needed (besides Fix) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Densetsu | 28 | SM01, Sanc02a, Sanc03 |
| 8 | Armael | 36 | PRock03 |
| 9 | XIII | 36 | Hual01b |
| 13 | John | 12 | Plage00 |
| 14 | Shinyaku | 10 | PRock04b |
