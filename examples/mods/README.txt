Example mods shipped with OpenXIII
==================================

None of these mods is active: the game never loads the _Examples folder.

To try one, copy its folder (for example "medkit-crate") from _Examples into the Mods folder,
next to _Examples, then start the game. It is then listed on the Mods page of the main menu,
where it can be turned off and on again. To remove it, delete its copy in Mods.

  beach-weapons     Beach: XIII starts with a pistol, a sniper rifle lies next to him, the
                    throwing knives are magnums (start weapons, add, remove, replace).
  blaster-gun       A new weapon with its own model, texture, sounds and ammo.
  flamethrower      A flamethrower on every level, with fire that spreads (Lua scripts).
  guard-dogs        Guard dogs brought to life with Lua scripts only.
  medkit-crate      A 3D model made for the mod, standing on the beach as a med kit.
  singing-soldier   Keys that make singing soldiers appear (characters, animation, sound).
  stronger-pistol   A 9mm pistol with a bigger magazine and more damage.
  tougher-soldiers  Soldiers with more health.
  xbox-recaps       The Xbox version's comic bubbles on the loading screen between two levels
                    (a Lua script: the game's own texts, a press to start).

The modding guide, the names catalog and the lists of names are in _Guide, beside this folder.
